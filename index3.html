Sometimes the L2 mempool accepts the sponsor's tx and then drops it, so nothing ever mines. The way around it is the chain's L1 delayed inbox: the same calls travel as mainnet transactions and are executed later by the sponsor's L2 alias, which pays its own L2 gas. The sponsor only pays mainnet gas here and no L2 tx is sent at all. Slower: the inbox waits a few minutes before it executes.
